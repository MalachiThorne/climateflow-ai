# ClimateFlow AI — Business Plan

## Mission
AI-powered operations for HVAC companies. We handle leads, reviews, and follow-ups so techs stay in the field.

## Target Market
- HVAC companies with 5-50 employees
- Owner-operated, doing $500k-$5M revenue
- Limited office staff, missing calls and leads
- Located in US metro areas (start with Pacific Northwest, expand)

## Services & Pricing

### 1. Lead Rescue — $1,500/mo
- AI answers missed calls via text-back within 60 seconds
- Qualifies leads (service type, urgency, address, availability)
- Books appointments directly into their calendar/CRM
- Handles after-hours and weekend inquiries
- Weekly lead report with conversion metrics

### 2. Review Autopilot — $500/mo
- Automated review request via text after completed jobs
- AI responds to all Google and Yelp reviews (positive and negative)
- Monthly reputation report with rating trends
- Escalates negative reviews to owner before responding

### 3. Estimate Follow-Up — $1,000/mo
- AI texts/emails customers with open estimates on a cadence
- Personalized follow-ups (not generic templates)
- Handles objections and questions via AI
- Alerts owner when estimate is accepted or needs attention
- Tracks close rate and average time-to-close

### Bundle — $2,500/mo
All three services. 4 clients = $10,000/mo.

## Revenue Targets
- Week 1-2: Build + launch outreach
- Week 3-4: Close first 2 clients ($5k MRR)
- Week 5-8: Close 2 more clients ($10k MRR)
- Month 3+: Scale to 10 clients ($25k MRR), hire VA for onboarding

## Tech Stack
- **Landing Page:** Next.js on Vercel
- **Automations:** Node.js + Twilio (SMS/voice) + OpenAI/Claude API
- **CRM/Scheduling:** Cal.com or Calendly integration
- **Outreach:** Custom scraper + email pipeline
- **Monitoring:** Dashboard for client metrics

## Costs (monthly)
- Twilio: ~$50-100/client
- AI API: ~$20-50/client
- Vercel: $20
- Domain + email: $15
- Total per client: ~$100-150
- Margin per client: ~$2,350-$2,400 (94%+)

## Sales Strategy
1. Cold email HVAC companies with personalized audit of their online presence
2. Offer free 7-day trial of Lead Rescue
3. Show them exactly how many leads they missed in that week
4. Close on the data
